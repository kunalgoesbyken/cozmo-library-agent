# Results

Status: no official ground-truth sheet exists yet. `ground_truth/` holds only the templates, so seven of the eight pass bars below are NOT EVALUATED. Nothing in this file is a pass claim except the time bar, and that one is for a three-photo batch, not a real sweep.

## How to produce the official results

1. Fill `ground_truth/books.csv`, `spines20.csv`, `prices15.csv`, `items.csv` and `room.json` by hand from the templates (formats, matching rules and thresholds: `ground_truth/README.md` and `config/eval.yaml`).
2. Run a real sweep, then:

```
python tools/eval.py runs/<id> ground_truth/ > docs/results.md
```

The generator rewrites this whole file. Keep the sections below only if you re-add them by hand.

## Generated by `tools/eval.py runs/f3-live ground_truth/` (unmodified apart from heading levels)

The run directory is `runs/f3-live` (three photos, not in git). The `_busy` rows and the "total (stage sum)" row add up task time across concurrent work; the wall time after the sweep is the 106.0 s row.

Run: `f3-live`

How the bars are read in this project (the brief gives tolerances, not how to aggregate them):

- Book count: error within 5.0% of the ground-truth row count.
- Titles: correct identifications over legible ground-truth spines at least 70.0%, and confidently wrong over all identified books at most 3.0%.
- Spine dimensions: the share of all 20 hand-measured spines within 15.0% must be at least 80.0% and the mean error at most 15.0%, for height and thickness. Unmatched spines and missing predictions count as failures; fewer than the required spines means not evaluated.
- Prices: over rows with the same source type, share within 25.0% at least 80.0% and mean error at most 25.0%; at least 10 comparable rows are required, otherwise not evaluated.
- Items: at least 80.0% found with the correct category, from at least 8 items.
- Floor within 10.0%, wall within 15.0%.
- Time: seconds after the sweep ended under 300.
